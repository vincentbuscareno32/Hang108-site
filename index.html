How it works

One tool. Four functions.

Function 01
Apply wax

Remove the cap and drag the opening face across your board. The twist dial advances the full-length wax bar as you go. No mess, no waste, no sandy bar rattling around your bag.

Function 02
Scrape old wax

Snap the cap onto the housing body. Now you have a full-grip scraper with real leverage. The concave edge follows your board's rocker. Long strokes, clean removal.

Function 03
Comb & texture

A full-length comb rail is molded into the side of the housing. Flip it to the side and drag. More teeth, longer stroke, faster texture than any standalone wax comb available.

Function 04
Tighten your fins

The twist dial detaches completely and works as a dual-tip fin key — 3/16" hex for Futures on one end, Phillips for FCS on the other. Universal. Always on you.
